# Nexora---App
Nexora is a personalized opportunity-discovery platform for independent financial advisors currently operating in the Tampa area. Advisors complete a short profile describing their experience, current and ideal clients, location, and weekly prospecting goals. Nexora then identifies recent opportunities, starting with new homeowners, newly registered businesses, and relevant networking events. It then ranks them based on factors such as advisor fit, recency, relevance, and location. Instead of overwhelming advisors with a generic list of leads, Nexora provides a small, prioritized feed of opportunities and explains why each one is relevant to that specific advisor.
